Who we are

A modular creative agency.

Creative because we work across several areas at once. Modular because each one is independent: hired on its own, working on its own, and connecting with the rest when it makes sense.

Wherever we're needed

80% remote, with clients in LatAm, Spain and the US. When we have to be there, we are.

The third way

Building a brand usually offers two uncomfortable options: an agency that charges for the whole project up front, or loose providers who don't talk to each other. LQS is the third way: take the piece you need today, pay for that, and add the next when you can.

And inside, the same

For each client we build the team that work needs. Everyone handling more than one area is on purpose: it lets us build the team around the case, instead of forcing the case into whichever area happened to be free.
