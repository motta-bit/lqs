Who we are

We are not just a creative agency.
We are a modular creative agency.

Creative because we work across several areas at once. Modular because each one stands alone: it is hired on its own, it works on its own, and it connects to the others when that makes sense. That word is not decoration — it is how we charge, deliver and grow.

We are wherever we are needed.

80% of the work is remote and we have clients in Latin America, Spain and the United States. When someone has to be there in person, we are.

Why it exists

A brand that needs a presence usually has two roads, and both are uncomfortable: an agency that charges for the whole project up front, or a handful of separate suppliers who do not talk to each other and each bill on their own.

LQS is the third road. You take the piece you need today, you pay for that one, and you add the next when you can.

And inside, the same

For each client we put together the team that the work needs, with the people who handle what has to be done. We do not hand out the same mould or assign a client to one area and leave them there.

Everyone handling more than one area is deliberate: it is what lets us build the team around the case, instead of pushing the case into whichever area happens to be free.
