The quote builder gives estimates, not offers

This is the most important part of this page, so it goes without detours:

The number that comes out of the quote builder is an estimate calculated on «from» rates. It is not a commercial offer within the meaning of article 845 of the Colombian Commercial Code and it does not bind LQS to anything.

The firm price comes from a written proposal, with the scope defined, which we send you after we talk. If a fault in the site shows a wrong figure, that figure does not bind us either: we correct it and let you know.

The same goes for published rates: they are the ones in force on the date of this page and may change without prior notice. The rate that governs is the one in the proposal we sign, not the one that was on the site the day you looked.

How hiring works

After we talk we send you a written proposal with scope, deliverables, timeline and price. Work starts when you accept that proposal in writing and the first payment is made.

  • Payments: 50% up front and 50% on delivery, unless the proposal says otherwise. The Social area is paid in advance with a fixed billing date.
  • Revisions: two rounds per delivery, agreed from the brief.
  • Timelines: they run from the moment you approve the brief and hand over the material needed. If material arrives late, the date moves by the same amount.
  • Rush work: under five business days carries a surcharge, and it is in the proposal.

If we miss the agreed date, we tell you straight away with a new one and you are compensated under the contract. It is not a goodwill gesture: it is written down.

Who owns the work

  • Economic rights in the final pieces transfer to the client once payment is complete. Before that, they remain with LQS.
  • Moral rights stay with whoever created the piece. In Colombia they are inalienable and cannot be waived (Law 23 of 1982); they cannot be transferred even if both parties want to.
  • Working files —editable projects, layers, edit sessions— are not handed over unless agreed and quoted separately. What is delivered are the finished pieces in the agreed formats.
  • Rejected proposals and discarded drafts belong to LQS and may not be used.
  • We may show the work in our portfolio, on social media and in proposals, unless we agree otherwise in writing. If you need confidentiality, say so before we start and it goes into the contract.
  • Material you hand over —logos, photos, text, music, fonts— has to be yours or licensed for the use we are going to give it. If it is not, any claims are yours to answer, and we say that warmly but seriously.

Where it actually ends up

It is worth knowing, because almost nobody says it: this site has no database and no server of its own. When you press send, the site composes the message and opens it in your WhatsApp or your email. You are the one who sends it, from your own account.

That means your message ends up where your messages end up: on WhatsApp, operated by Meta, or in email, operated by Google. Those companies store information on servers outside Colombia and have their own privacy policies. That is an international transfer of data and we tell you so you can decide knowing it.

If you would rather use neither, write to the physical address above.

  • Clients: for as long as the relationship lasts, and then for as long as commercial and tax rules require records to be kept.
  • When you ask for deletion and no legal duty obliges us to keep it, it is deleted.

Cookie policy

This site uses no cookies. Not session, not analytics, not advertising. None.

What that means concretely

  • There is no analytics. No Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no measurement tool of any kind. We do not know how many people come in or where from.
  • There is no advertising and no remarketing. This site will not follow you around the internet.
  • We store nothing in your browser. No cookies, no localStorage, no sessionStorage.
  • What you pick in the quote builder travels in the page address, not on your device. That is why you can copy the link and send it to someone with the selection inside — and why, when you close the tab, nothing is left behind.
  • Your language choice works the same way. English lives on its own pages under /en/. Nothing is saved to remember it.

That is why you will not see a cookie banner. We are not hiding it and we did not skip it: there is simply nothing to consent to. A window asking permission for nothing would be theatre.

The only third-party request

The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. That installs no cookies, but it does show Google your IP address and the browser you use, because it is your device that goes and asks for the file.

It is the only connection to a third party this page makes, and it can be removed by hosting the typefaces on the site itself. It is on our to-do list and when it is done, we update this.

When you leave the site

Tapping «Send on WhatsApp» or an email link takes you out of here. From that moment the cookies and policies of WhatsApp, your email provider or your browser apply. We do not control them.

If this changes

If we ever add measurement, we say so here before doing it, with a date, explaining what is measured and what for — and if that measurement collects personal data, we ask for your authorisation, as Law 1581 of 2012 requires.

Colombia has no specific cookie law. When a cookie collects personal data, the general regime of Law 1581 of 2012 applies to it. We say so to be clear about which rule we are under, not to hide behind it.
