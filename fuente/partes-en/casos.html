Case 01 · NGO · 2026
Asociación
Vita Nova
Colombia

From scattered information to a single front door

An inclusion and education NGO based in Medellín, operating in four hubs across the country. Institutional information sat in loose files and enrolments came in by hand, one at a time.

LQS built the institutional site —bilingual, with a high-contrast mode in line with Colombian Law 1581—, the dossier for partners and institutions, and a cascading enrolment form that delivers every record by email without anyone transcribing it. We have run their social accounts since.

17departments and municipalities covered by the form
60+technical programmes and 14 degrees linked
4areas connected into one ecosystem
