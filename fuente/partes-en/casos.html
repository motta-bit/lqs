What we did with Vita Nova

NGO · inclusion · since 2026 Asociación Vita Nova Colombia

From scattered information to a single front door

Vita Nova is an inclusion and education association based in Medellín, operating in four hubs across the country. Its information sat in loose files and enrolments came in by hand, one at a time.

LQS built its institutional site —bilingual, with a high-contrast mode in line with Colombian Law 1581—, the dossier for partners, and a cascading enrolment form that delivers every record by email without anyone transcribing it. We have run their social accounts since.
